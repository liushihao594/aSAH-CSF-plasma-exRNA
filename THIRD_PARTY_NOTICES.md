The XDec method/code and reference resources are associated with Murillo et al. and the BRL-BCM/XDec repository. No third-party XDec code, reference matrix or sample-level proportion output is bundled. `xdec_technical_checks_no_deconvolution.R` is a paper-specific feasibility checker that requires users to obtain any third-party inputs separately. It does not perform or claim the formal sample-level carrier analysis.
